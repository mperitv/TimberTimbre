<h1 align="center">TimberTimbre Devlog Day 1</h1>

#### Hi, This is 1st devlog of TimberTimbre!  
This project will be a clock with bunch of features.  
This idea came about accidentally
when I found my old mouse and wanted to open it up.  
I was interested in hardware when I was 10,
I built cool projects with Arduino. But today,
my hardware skills are gone and I can't even use
soldering iron properly anymore!
But nowadays, I'm just building software projects and
I'm kinda bored so I'll do some hardware stuff because sometimes
it's more engaging to build projects that lives in real life, not in a computer.
### Today I...
- Opened the mouse and took out the PCB
- Desoldered all the components from the PCB
- Took out the components from my toolbox 
- Built an enclosure for a 16x2 LCD panel out of craft sticks
- Hot-glued the craft sticks using a lighter
- Tested whether the LCD screen works or not
- Found the hot glue gun and glued the craft sticks for the base.
- Hot-glued them to the enclosure holding the LCD screen
